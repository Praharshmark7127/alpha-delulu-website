Service 01

Software & apps

What it means: we design, build and test the software your business needs: apps for your customers, tools for your team, and systems that keep all your information in one place.

Good for you if…

  • You have an app or website idea but no tech team
  • Your team is stuck juggling spreadsheets
  • Your current software keeps breaking

What you get

  • Mobile apps for Android and iPhone
  • Business systems (ERP) for stock, billing and staff
  • Proper testing before anything goes live
  • Dashboards that show your numbers clearly
Service 02

Networks & cloud

What it means: networks are the roads your data travels on. We design them, set them up and keep them fast, safe and online, including 5G, the cloud and smart automation.

Good for you if…

  • Your internet or systems keep going down
  • You're moving to the cloud and don't know where to start
  • You want boring, repeat tasks done automatically

What you get

  • Network planning and setup
  • 5G and telecom engineering
  • Cloud setup and moving your data safely
  • Automation and AI tools that save time
Service 03

Skilled tech people

The tech world calls this "staff augmentation".

What it means: sometimes you don't need a whole project, you need people. We add trained tech experts to your team, and they work just like your own staff.

Good for you if…

  • You have a deadline and not enough hands
  • You need a skill your team doesn't have
  • Hiring full-time takes too long or costs too much

What you get

  • Developers, network engineers, testers and project managers
  • People ready to start quickly
  • Short or long contracts, your choice
  • We handle the HR and paperwork
